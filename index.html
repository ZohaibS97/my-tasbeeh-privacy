Privacy Policy for My Tasbeeh

Effective date: 3 October 2026

My Tasbeeh is a digital Tasbeeh counter application designed to help users keep track of their daily dhikr, set targets, save progress, and receive optional reminders.

Information We Collect

My Tasbeeh does not require users to create an account and does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or payment information.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores information locally on your device so that your progress and preferences can be remembered.

This may include:

  • Tasbeeh names
  • Tasbeeh counts
  • Target counts
  • Recent activity timestamps
  • Sound preferences
  • Vibration preferences
  • Left- or right-hand layout preference
  • Reminder settings and reminder time

This information is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

Notifications

My Tasbeeh can optionally send daily reminder notifications.

If you enable reminders, the app requests permission from your device to display notifications.

Notification permissions are used only for the reminder feature.

Data Sharing

My Tasbeeh does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or other third parties.

The app does not currently contain advertising, analytics services, user accounts, or cloud-based data storage.

Data Retention

Your Tasbeeh data and preferences remain stored locally on your device until you change or delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the application.

Because the app does not maintain user accounts or store your Tasbeeh data on an external server, there is no remote account or cloud data to delete.

Security

My Tasbeeh is designed to minimise data collection. Tasbeeh information is stored locally on the user's device rather than being transmitted to an external application server.

Children's Privacy

My Tasbeeh does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change.
